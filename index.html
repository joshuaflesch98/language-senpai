 • / — both fully correct, either counts: lopen = laufen / gehen
 • [ ] — valid but not the word being drilled: shows teal and asks you to type the target instead. Add as many as you need: dichtbij [nabij] [vlakbij] = nahe
 • ( ) — something extra that's never required: shown as a hint when it's in the question, accepted but not needed when it's in your answer: (zich) wassen accepts "wassen" and "zich wassen"
